## Abstract

Logical relations are a proof technique for establishing properties of programs and programming languages, such as termination, type soundness, equivalence, and noninterference. In this talk, we review the classic semantic type soundness proof for STLC based on logical relations, then generalise it to bidirectional STLC, and finally extend logical relations further to cover all syntactically valid terms: not only well-typed ones, but also ill-typed and even ill-scoped terms.
