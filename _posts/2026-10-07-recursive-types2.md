Recursive types are pervasive in programming languages and compiler
infrastructures, from algebraic data types in functional languages to
intermediate representations in modern compilers. However, checking subtyping
between recursive types remains challenging. Traditional algorithms like Amber
rules require backtracking, leading to performance bottlenecks at scale.

This talk is focused on presenting **QuickSub**, an efficient iso-recursive
subtyping algorithm that checks subtyping in a single traversal and exhibits
linear-time performance in practice. I will also discuss my other work and
future directions in recursive types metatheory and their applications to
programming languages.
